Make repayments effortless

A customer in a far barangay pays in seconds instead of losing half a day and a fare. Convenience drives on-time payment — the cheapest NPL reduction there is.

Grow the book without growing headcount

Faster turnaround captures borrowers who would otherwise go to high-interest lenders, and the small-ticket tier you could never staff becomes volume.

Origination

Take loan applications digitally.

Borrowers apply from a phone. Branch staff work the same queue. Loan officers capture applications on a tablet in the field. Documents, KYC, and credit-committee approvals move through one system with one audit trail.

  • Digital application flows — no manual paperwork. Capture digital loan applications for high-velocity tiers instantly and give your customers a fast, modern borrowing experience from anywhere.
  • Electronic KYC and document capture — photo capture and identity verification
  • Configurable approval workflows — mirror your existing credit committee and delegation limits
  • Full audit trail — every action, every approver, every timestamp, examination-ready
Underwriting and Scoring

Your credit policy, applied consistently.

You set the rules. Higala applies them the same way on every application. As repayments run through the platform, the scoring layer learns from behaviour you can see and audit — never from a model you can't inspect.

  • Rules engine you control — thresholds, ratios, exclusions, and overrides configured by your credit team
  • Behavioural repayment signals — on-time and late patterns from your own borrowers
  • Consistent decisions — the same inputs produce the same outcome regardless of who keys them
  • Portfolio and vintage views — see how each cohort performs, per product and per branch
  • Board and BSP reporting — pull the numbers instead of assembling them
Servicing and Disbursement

Release funds and track them from the same place.

Approve a loan and disburse it to the borrower's bank account or e-wallet over InstaPay. The disbursement, the schedule, and the ledger entry are one event, not three handoffs.

  • Real-time disbursement to any InstaPay-reachable account or wallet
  • Automated amortization schedules per product terms
  • Restructuring and rescheduling handled in-system with the audit trail intact
  • Arrears and ageing views updated as payments settle, not overnight
Collections

Repayments that reconcile themselves.

This is where the manual work goes. Higala connects your institution to InstaPay through RCBC as sponsor bank. Borrowers pay from any bank or e-wallet in the country. Funds credit your account in real time, carry a reference your system matches on the first pass, and post against the right loan without anyone opening a spreadsheet.

  • Pay-in from any Philippine bank or e-wallet — meet borrowers where their money already is. Since BSP Circular 1238 took effect in 2026, most major banks and e-wallets charge nothing for InstaPay transfers, so paying you costs the borrower nothing.

  • Real-time settlement visibility — every payment traceable end to end
